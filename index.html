About Me

I am a Bachelor of Science in Architecture graduate with a passion for creating functional, sustainable, and user-centered spaces. Skilled in architectural design, drafting, and 3D visualization, I enjoy transforming ideas into practical design solutions. As I begin my professional career, I am committed to continuous learning, collaboration, and contributing to meaningful architectural projects.

I believe that architecture should actively support the well-being of its users through thoughtful layout choices, intuitive wayfinding, and balanced spatial flows.
