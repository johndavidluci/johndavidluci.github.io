About Me

I am an architectural designer focused on crafting efficient, inclusive spaces. My approach combines meticulous programmatic zoning—ensuring circulation routes, service lanes, and monitoring levels function seamlessly—with strict adherence to regional accessibility codes.

I believe that healthcare and community architecture should actively support the well-being of its users through thoughtful layout choices, intuitive wayfinding, and balanced spatial flows.
